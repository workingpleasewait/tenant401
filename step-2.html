Notify the Landlord by Certified Mail

Send a certified letter to the owner before you file with DHCR. Keep everything — you will attach it to your DHCR application.

How to send

Go to any USPS post office. Ask for Certified Mail with Return Receipt. You will get a green card back when they sign for it — keep that too.

Mail to:
205 Realty Holdings LLC
1274 49th Street, Suite 175
Brooklyn, NY 11219
Use this sample letter
[Your Name] 205 East 17th Street, Apt. [Your Unit] Brooklyn, NY 11226 [Date] 205 Realty Holdings LLC 1274 49th Street, Suite 175 Brooklyn, NY 11219 Dear 205 Realty Holdings LLC, I am writing to formally request that the elevator service at 205 East 17th Street be restored immediately. As of [date], the elevator has been [describe: completely out of service / unreliable / malfunctioning]. Despite prior complaints, the service remains unreliable. I request prompt repairs and a written response confirming the timeline. If this is not resolved within a reasonable time, I will file a rent reduction application with DHCR. Sincerely, [Your Name] [Your Phone / Email]
Before mailing
  • Fill in your name, unit, date, and description of the problem
  • Sign the letter
  • Make a copy for yourself before you seal the envelope
  • Send via Certified Mail — keep the receipt
  • Keep the green return receipt card when it comes back
📋 Save these
  • A copy of the signed letter
  • The certified mail receipt (from the post office)
  • The green return receipt card (when it arrives)

You will attach all of these to your DHCR Form RA-84 in Step 3.
