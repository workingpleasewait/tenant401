Rent reduction rights for lack of elevator service

Is this your building? This guide is for rent-stabilized and rent-controlled tenants at 205 East 17th Street, Brooklyn. The DHCR process described here applies to rent-regulated apartments only.
Emergency? If someone is trapped, an elevator shaft is open, or the elevator is moving dangerously — call 911, not 311.

Elevator out of service?

You may be entitled to a rent reduction. This three-step process worked for a tenant here — a formal DHCR rent reduction order was issued in 2024 that continues today.

Three steps
1 File a 311 Complaint Create an official city record of the elevator problem › 2 Notify the Landlord Send a certified letter — sample letter included › 3 File with DHCR — Form RA-84 The official application for a rent reduction ›
Real-world result
A tenant at this building filed — and won.
  • Complaint filed: April 16, 2024
  • Certified letter sent to 205 Realty Holdings LLC
  • DHCR application submitted with all attachments
  • Rent reduction effective August 1, 2024
  • DHCR order issued October 11, 2024
  • Docket: MP 210039 B

The order is still in effect. Two landlord restoration attempts have been denied — the most recent in August 2026. Other tenants at this building can use the same process.
