3

File with DHCR — Form RA-84

Use Form RA-84. Mail the original and one copy of the signed form, plus one copy of each attachment.

Download Form RA-84 → hcr.ny.gov/form-ra-84

Other tenants joining? Also attach Form RA-84.1.

Be specific: Describe every elevator problem you actually experienced — not only a complete outage. DHCR's later restoration review focuses on the conditions covered by its order.
What to attach
  • Signed Form RA-84 (original) + one copy
  • Copy of your certified letter to the landlord
  • Certified mail receipt; return card if received
  • Your 311 Service Request numbers or screenshots
Mail to:
DHCR – Rent Administration Office
Gertz Plaza, 92-31 Union Hall Street, 6th Floor
Jamaica, NY 11433

A complete copy of your filing and the docket number DHCR mails to you.
