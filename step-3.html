3

File with DHCR — Form RA-84

Use Form RA-84 for building-wide service failures like an elevator. Mail the original signed form plus one copy of every attachment. Keep a complete copy for yourself.

Download Form RA-84 → hcr.ny.gov/form-ra-84

If other tenants in the building are joining the same complaint, also attach Form RA-84.1.

What to attach
  • Signed Form RA-84 (original) + one copy
  • Copy of your certified letter to the landlord
  • Certified mail receipt (from the post office)
  • Return receipt card (if received — include if you have it)
  • Your 311 Service Request number or screenshot
Mail to:
DHCR – Rent Administration Office
Gertz Plaza, 92-31 Union Hall Street, 6th Floor
Jamaica, NY 11433
📋 Save these
  • A complete copy of everything you mailed
  • Your DHCR docket number — DHCR sends an acknowledgement by mail after filing

You will need the docket number to check your case status.

Track your case: HCR Tenant Resources (hcr.ny.gov)

Real-world result
A tenant at this building filed — and won.
