File a 311 Complaint

Report the problem at 311.nyc.gov or call 311. The elevator does not have to stop running. Describe exactly what happened.

What to report

Report it if the elevator:

  • Bounces, jolts, jerks, or stops roughly
  • Stops above or below the floor, or drifts after stopping
  • Has doors that fail to open, close, or reopen properly
  • Has broken buttons, alarms, or emergency communication
  • Stalls, skips floors, shuts down, or works intermittently

These are examples, not a complete list. Report every condition you actually experience — even one hard bounce.
