File a 311 Complaint

Go to portal.311.nyc.gov or call 311. Be specific about what you see — DHCR can only rule on conditions you describe in your complaint.

What to report

Describe the elevator problem in your own words. Examples:

  • "Elevator not functioning properly"
  • "Elevator bounces when stopping — trip hazard"
  • "Elevator car stops several inches below floor level"
  • "Elevator completely out of service"

The more specific you are, the stronger your record.

📋 Save this

After filing you will receive a Service Request (SR) number.

  • Write it down or take a screenshot
  • You will attach it to your DHCR application in Step 3
If the elevator breaks again — file a new 311 complaint. Each report creates a new dated record of the condition. Save every new SR number.
